About

I’m Sasha Mindrin, an independent designer. I work on visual communication end to end: I find the idea behind a brand and carry it through the logo, the motion, the website and the interactive pieces — so everything a person sees speaks with one voice.

I do both the thinking and the making. The concept, the design and the working result come from the same hands, which keeps the idea intact from first sketch to launch.

how I work

  • Concept first. Every project starts with research and one core idea. You see it and approve it before any production begins.
  • One system, many forms. Logo and identity, motion, website, interactive — as much of it as the project needs.
  • A few projects a year. A typical engagement runs one to four months, so each one gets full attention.
  • Remote, in English. I work with clients worldwide. Payment by invoice in USD or EUR.

Have a project in mind? Tell me what you’re building and when you need it — I’ll get back to you soon.
